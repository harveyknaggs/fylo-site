What you'd do

Four steps, then you're done.

No phone system to replace. No scripts to write. One carrier setting and three minutes of setup is all it takes.

1
Forward your number on busy or no-answer

One setting in your carrier's app or a single USSD code. Works on any mobile plan. Voice gives you the exact steps for your carrier.

2
Record a 10-second voice sample or pick a preset

Voice learns your cadence from a short recording. Your greeting, your pace. Or choose a neutral professional preset and skip straight to setup.

3
Set your business hours and services

Voice only books into the slots you make available. Add your service list so it knows what to offer and what to escalate to you directly.

4
Set your escalation rule

Pick the keywords or call types that should wake you up: "urgent", "damage", "cancel". Voice texts you immediately when one comes in.

That's it. Voice handles every missed call from here. No further input needed.
