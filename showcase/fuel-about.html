What you'd do

Four steps, then you're done.

Nothing to install in the trucks. Connect a card feed, tell Fuel about your fleet, and it sorts every fill from there.

1
Connect your fuel cards

Link Z, Mobil, BP, Gull or any provider with a card feed. Read-only access, takes about a minute.

2
Add your vehicles and cards

List your regos and which card lives in which vehicle. Fuel uses this to match every fill automatically.

3
Set your coding rules

Map fuel to the right GL accounts and tracking categories so it codes the way your accountant expects.

4
Set your spend alerts

Pick the thresholds that matter: a fill over $200, two fills in an hour, a card used off-hours. Fuel texts you.

That's it. Every fill sorts itself from here. No more month-end guesswork.
